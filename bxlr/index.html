BXLR

DJ SET / LIVE
Emerging from an anonymous beginning six years ago, BXLR was conceived as a shadow project driven purely by sonic exploration and emotional intensity. Deeply influenced by a wide spectrum of techno subgenres throughout his life, he gradually shaped a personal language rooted in dark, ominous sound design and cinematic tension. Drawing inspiration from early Industrial Hardcore movements such as The Relic and Cubic Nomad, BXLR blends heavy, desperate waveforms with detailed FX work and cinematic structures. His sound stands at the intersection of industrial techno and atmospheric storytelling, where pressure, distortion, and mood coexist in a raw yet controlled balance.

Biography

As a self-taught producer, BXLR began releasing music in 2023, quickly building a solid catalog of 17 tracks circulating within the underground scene. His work has reached respected platforms, including a release on the NO SLEEP label, reinforcing his identity as an artist committed to uncompromising sound and continuous evolution. Parallel to his studio work, BXLR has brought his vision to the dancefloor through performances at venues such as Hyper Club and Molotov Club in Vigo, Spain. His DJ sets are conceived as dark, immersive journeys, breaking conventional structures with hard-driving drums, relentless energy, and dense atmospheric layers. With the ability to perform live using the core elements of his own productions, BXLR fully dissolves the barrier between creator and performance. The result is a visceral, enveloping experience that pulls the listener deep into the essence of industrial techno, where intensity, darkness, and emotion remain at the forefront.
