Club History

Die Nacht Brennt Club was born from the urge to create an authentic space for underground techno in Galicia. Since its inception, the club has united artists, ravers, and minds who crave something different. Always moving forward, always pushing the boundaries, DNBC is a community and a movement as much as a club.

From clandestine warehouse nights to open-air raves, our story is one of passion, rebellion, and a relentless love for the music that brings us together.

Evolution & Values

DNBC stands for community, freedom, and the raw energy of the dancefloor. We believe in inclusivity, self-expression, and breaking down barriers between artist and crowd.

Over the years, the club has grown into an agency, label, and platform for new generations of electronic music lovers. Our mission is to keep the flame burning for underground culture—one night, one track, one memory at a time.

[ Resident DJ ]

People Make the Club

Our residents, guest DJs, and the crowd are all part of the same experience. Every event is a collaboration between everyone in the room.

DNBC is not just a place—it's a feeling that stays with you long after the lights come up.
