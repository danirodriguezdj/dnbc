Club History

Die Nacht Brennt Club was born from the urge to create an authentic space for underground techno in Galicia. Since its inception, the club has united artists, ravers, and minds who crave something different.

From warehouse nights to open-air raves, our story is one of rebellion and relentless love for music.

Evolution & Values

DNBC stands for community, freedom, and raw energy. We believe in inclusivity, self-expression, and breaking down barriers. Over the years, the club has grown into an agency, label, and platform for new generations of electronic music lovers.

[ Resident DJ ]

People Make the Club

Our residents, guest DJs, and the crowd form one experience. Every event is a collective ritual. DNBC is not just a place—it’s a feeling.
