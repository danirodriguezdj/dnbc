Giulia Nigro

ARTIST MANAGEMENT

Giulia Nigro began her journey in the underground music scene as a dedicated raver, deeply immersed in the trance and psytrance world. Her passion for music and community soon led her to take an active role behind the scenes, working in public relations and artist care for techno and tech house events. Even at a young age, she stood out for her professionalism, responsibility, and natural ability to connect with artists and promoters alike.

Her deep understanding of underground culture, combined with a strong work ethic and a genuine love for what she does, makes her a respected figure in the scene. Giulia isn’t just part of the industry, she’s emotionally invested in every project she joins, driven by authenticity, consistency, and a clear vision of how to support and grow the culture she believes in.

Biography

In 2023, she found her true place in the Hard Techno scene. Her role quickly evolved from PR to a more strategic and impactful position, handling bookings, artist logistics, and long-term career support.

By 2024, Giulia was recognized as a key player in the ecosystem, known for building strong, trust-based relationships and always advocating for the artists she works with.
